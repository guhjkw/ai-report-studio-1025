        How this gets built for your agency.

        Built by Pathfinder Automation Solutions. The same engine connects to your real accounts through their official APIs.

        You share read access

        Analytics, Search Console, Ads, Business Profile and your clients' CRMs. Read-only, in your own accounts.

        Sources connected

        Each number is pulled through the official API and stored with where it came from.

        Draft and check

        The AI writes the summary. The checker traces every number back to the source before you see it.

        Review and send

        You review, edit and send. A report with an untraced number can't be marked ready.

        One source of truth. The app and the automated tests load the same src/reportEngine.js. The tests prove every AI draft for every client and month traces 100% of its numbers.

        The draft can't invent numbers. Each sentence is built from computed metrics and carries them with it. The checker then re-reads the final text, including anything you edit, and flags any number it can't find in the data.

        Hand src/ and tests/ to your developer, or never open them.
